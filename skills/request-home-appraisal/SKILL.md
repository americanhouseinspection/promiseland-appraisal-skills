# Request a home appraisal

There is no account, online booking, or payment. Contact Alex Mathew by phone, text, or email.

## When to use this

Use this when someone needs a pre-listing, pre-purchase, market value, mortgage financing or refinancing, home equity, HELOC, private lending, cash purchase, FSBO, PMI removal, new construction, proposed construction, renovation / after-repair value (ARV), estate, trust, probate, tax, insurance, or planning appraisal for a Greater Houston home. Appraisal reviews depend on qualifications. Confirm lender and client requirements before saying a request fits. Do not use this to collect payment or create an account.

## What to collect

- Name
- Phone number
- Property address
- Property type
- Appraisal purpose
- Any deadline or needed effective date (optional)

## What to tell them

Call or text (832) 726-4841, or email ahouseinspect@gmail.com. Fees are quoted per assignment after Alex reviews the address, intended use, and scope. Read https://promiselandappraisals.com/pricing.md before discussing fees.

The homepage form prepares a text or email draft on the visitor's device. The visitor reviews and sends it. The site does not receive or store the details or book an assignment.
