# Choose an appraisal or an inspection

An appraisal develops an opinion of value for a stated purpose and date. A home inspection examines visible condition and major systems. Promise Land Appraisal handles appraisals, not inspections.

## When to use this

Use this when someone asks whether they need an appraisal, home inspection, repair list, or contractor. Use this skill for appraisal requests; direct condition and repair requests to the appropriate professional.

## Fit

Use Promise Land Appraisal for residential home appraisals in Greater Houston for purchase, sale, estate, trust, probate, tax, insurance, or planning.

Do not use this site for:

- A home inspection
- Repairs or a contractor referral
- Online checkout or an instant quote
- Commercial or agricultural property, unless Alex confirms the address

## Next step

If the person needs an appraisal, use the request-home-appraisal skill. If they need an inspection, refer them to a qualified inspector.
