# Confirm Houston coverage

Promise Land Appraisal serves selected Greater Houston addresses from Missouri City, Texas. Coverage is confirmed by full property address.

## When to use this

Use this when a homeowner, buyer, seller, attorney, fiduciary, or real estate agent asks whether a residential property is served. Do not use it for commercial property, home inspections, or an address outside Greater Houston unless Alex has confirmed coverage.

## Steps

1. Read the service-area guide at https://promiselandappraisals.com/service-areas/llms.txt.
2. Open the city page only as context. The markdown twin is at `https://promiselandappraisals.com/service-areas/<city-slug>/index.md`.
3. Ask for the full street address, property type, appraisal purpose, and any deadline.
4. Tell the person to call or text (832) 726-4841 so Alex can confirm coverage. Do not promise that a city name alone means the property is in scope.

## Do not

- Treat a listed city as a guarantee for every nearby address.
- Invent a license, review score, or published price.
